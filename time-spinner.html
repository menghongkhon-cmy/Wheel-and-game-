What is a Time Spinner?

A Time Spinner is a random time generator that turns a range of clock times into a spinning wheel. Instead of manually picking a slot, you set a start time, an end time, and an interval — the tool builds every time in between and randomly lands on one, the same way a name wheel picks a random winner from a list.

How to use the Time Spinner

  1. Select a start time.
  2. Select an end time.
  3. Choose an interval (5, 10, 15, 30 minutes, or 1 hour).
  4. Click "Generate time slots".
  5. Press "Spin" and watch the wheel.
  6. Get your random time — copy or share it instantly.

Frequently asked questions

What does the Time Spinner do?
It builds a list of evenly spaced time slots between a start and end time you choose, then spins a wheel to land on one at random.
Can I choose the interval between times?
Yes. Pick 5, 10, 15, or 30 minute steps, or a full 1 hour step, and the tool generates every slot in that range.
What happens if the range doesn't divide evenly?
The tool stops at the last slot that fits inside your end time, so you never get a time past the range you set.
Is the result actually random?
Yes. The winning slot is chosen using the browser's cryptographic random number generator before the wheel animation starts.
Does it save my results?
Your most recent results are saved in your browser's local storage so you can glance back at them, and nothing is sent to a server.
Can I use this for classroom or meeting scheduling?
Yes, it's commonly used to pick presentation slots, break times, or meeting start times fairly and quickly.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes, the wheel and controls are fully responsive and touch-friendly on phones, tablets, and desktops.
Is the Time Spinner free to use?
Yes, it's completely free with no sign-up required.
